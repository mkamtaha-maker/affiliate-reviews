Key Features Breakdown

1. ECE 22.06 & P/J Dual Homologation Standard

The Neotec 3 isn't just certified under the rigorous ECE 22.06 standard; it also boasts P/J dual homologation. This means it is officially approved and fully legal to ride with the chin bar locked in the open position as well as fully closed.

2. AIM Organic Fibre Shell Construction

Shoei's proprietary Advanced Integrated Matrix (AIM) combines organic fibres and multi-composite materials to create a shell that is incredibly shock-absorbent while maintaining optimum rigidity and lower weight.

3. Integrated QSV-2 Inner Sun Visor

Designed specifically for shifting light conditions, the QSV-2 internal drop-down visor offers extended coverage compared to its predecessor, blocking 99% of harmful UV rays without compromising the thickness of the inner EPS liner.

4. Prepared for Sena SRL3 Communication System

The helmet features seamless integration ports designed specifically for the optional Sena SRL3 comms system. Keep your ride streamlined without awkward external battery packs or high wind noise.

5. Advanced Multi-Channel Ventilation System

Engineered in Shoei's dedicated wind tunnel, the revamped ventilation features extractable air intakes at the brow and chin, combined with rear extractors to maximize airflow on warm days while minimizing fogging in cold UK winters.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Shoei Neotec 3 quiet at motorway speeds?

Yes. Shoei's wind-tunnel testing has produced re-engineered cheek pads and noise isolators, making it one of the quietest flip-up helmets available on the market today.

Can I fit a universal intercom like a Cardo to the Neotec 3?

While designed specifically for the Sena SRL3, universal clamp units can still be fitted with adapter mounts, though the dedicated Sena unit provides the cleanest aesthetic.
