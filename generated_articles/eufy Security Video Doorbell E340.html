eufy Security Video Doorbell E340: Complete UK Review

Smart video doorbells have become a staple for UK home security, but traditional single-lens models often suffer from a major flaw: blind spots right at your doorstep where parcels are left. The eufy Security Video Doorbell E340 addresses this challenge head-on with an innovative dual-camera configuration combined with local storage, eliminating both hidden blind spots and ongoing monthly subscription costs.

Build Quality and Dual Camera Design

Priced at £159.99, the eufy E340 feels robust and well-engineered to withstand unpredictable UK weather. It houses a primary 2K HD camera pointing forward to capture visitors' faces clearly, alongside a secondary 1080p downward-facing camera focused directly on the porch floor. This dual-lens setup ensures complete top-to-bottom visibility, making missed deliveries or hidden packages a thing of the past.

Performance, Color Night Vision, and Smart AI

Video quality is exceptionally sharp in both daylight and dark conditions. Thanks to built-in dual illumination spotlights, the camera delivers vibrant color night vision when motion is detected. The on-device AI accurately distinguishes between human movement, pets, and package drop-offs, sending precise mobile notifications without overwhelming you with false alerts from passing cars.

No Monthly Fees: Local Storage Advantage

One of the strongest selling points for UK consumers is eufy's commitment to subscription-free surveillance. Video recordings are stored locally on the unit or synced to an optional eufy HomeBase, meaning your data remains private and secure without requiring a £3 to £10 monthly cloud subscription fee.

Who Should Buy the eufy E340?

If you are a tech-savvy homeowner looking for reliable, high-definition front door coverage with zero ongoing costs, the eufy E340 is an absolute winner. It offers easy battery installation (with optional hardwiring) and seamlessly integrates with Alexa and Google Assistant setups.
