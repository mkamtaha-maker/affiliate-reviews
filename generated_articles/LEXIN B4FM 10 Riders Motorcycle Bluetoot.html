Key Features and Real-World Performance

10-Rider Group Conference Intercom

Unlike entry-level units restricted to simple rider-to-passenger chats, the LEXIN B4FM features universal pairing that connects up to 10 riders simultaneously. Whether you are leading a group ride through the countryside or commuting in tandem, the connection delivers clear line-of-sight communication.

Audio Clarity with DSP & CVC Noise Reduction

Riding at UK motorway speeds usually creates significant wind roar. Thanks to integrated DSP and CVC noise reduction algorithms, the high-definition speakers isolate your voice cleanly. Phone calls are clear, and hands-free voice commands via Siri or Google Assistant work reliably at the press of a button.

IP67 Waterproofing for British Weather

Riding in the UK means facing unpredictable downpours. The B4FM features a fully sealed IP67 water-resistant casing that resists heavy rain and road spray, ensuring long-term durability year-round.

Music Sharing and FM Radio Functionality

Long motorway stints are made enjoyable with built-in FM radio tuner and music sharing capabilities, allowing rider and pillion passenger to listen to the same playlist on the go.
