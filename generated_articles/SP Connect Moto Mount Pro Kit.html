Build Quality & Design

When securing a £1,000+ smartphone to a motorcycle handlebar, flimsy plastic clamps simply will not suffice. The SP Connect Moto Mount Pro sets a gold standard in build quality, precision CNC-machined from aircraft-grade aluminum. It offers an exceptionally sleek profile that seamlessly integrates into the cockpit of any machine, whether you ride an adventure bike, sports bike, or cruiser.

Vibration Dampening & Camera Protection

High-frequency motorcycle engine vibrations are notorious for destroying the delicate Optical Image Stabilization (OIS) systems inside modern flagship smartphones. The Moto Mount Pro directly addresses this issue with an integrated vibration dampening system. This specialized elastomeric insert attenuates harmful vibrations before they reach your phone, ensuring your camera hardware remains intact through long rides and spirited throttles.

Flexibility & Adjustability

Versatility is key when setting up motorcycle navigation. The Moto Mount Pro accommodates handlebar diameters ranging from 2.22cm to 3.17cm (7/8 to 1.25 inches), fitting almost all standard and oversized motorcycle setups. Furthermore, it boasts full 360-degree rotation capabilities in fine 6-degree increments, allowing you to seamlessly swap between portrait and landscape modes while fine-tuning your view to avoid glare.

Is It Worth Buying?

Priced at £64.99, the SP Connect Moto Mount Pro represents a worthy investment for riders who demand premium aesthetic integration alongside uncompromised physical security. If you value durable craftsmanship, compact design, and robust camera protection on UK roads, this setup delivers outstanding value.
