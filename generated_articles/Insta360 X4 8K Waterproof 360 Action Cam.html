Key Features Breakdown for Motorcycle Enthusiasts

1. Exceptional 8K 30fps 360-Degree Video Capture

The headline feature of the Insta360 X4 is its 8K capture capability. For motorcycle riders, higher resolution means you can refrome your shot during post-production without losing detail. Whether you want to focus on the scenic Scottish Highlands ahead or your leaning angle around tight bends, the reframed 1080p output remains crisp, clear, and sharp.

2. FlowState Stabilisation & 360 Horizon Lock

Vibrations from thumping single-cylinder engines or bumpy B-roads can ruin standard footage. The Insta360 X4 features industry-leading FlowState stabilisation. Paired with 360 Horizon Lock, your footage stays level and silky smooth regardless of how aggressively you lean into turns or hit uneven off-road tracks.

3. The Invisible Selfie Stick Effect

Mount the Insta360 X4 to your handlebars or tail kit using an invisible selfie stick, and the stitching software automatically erases the pole from your video. The result is incredible third-person, drone-like tracking footage without needing an actual drone pilot following your ride.

4. Built for British Weather: Rugged & Waterproof to 10m

Riding in the UK means facing sudden downpours. The Insta360 X4 boasts an IPX8 waterproof rating straight out of the box down to 10 metres. You no longer need to worry about quick weather changes or mud splatters during green laning adventures.

5. Advanced Wind Noise Reduction Algorithms

Wind buffeting is the enemy of clean motorcycling audio. The X4 incorporates upgraded wind noise reduction algorithms that filter out harsh rushing air sounds, making engine notes and speech noticeably clearer even at motorway speeds.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

How does the camera perform at high motorcycling speeds?

The high frame rate options (such as 5.7K at 60fps) alongside FlowState stabilisation ensure that motion blur is minimised and footage remains rock-steady even at high speeds on the motorway.

Is the lens scratch-resistant against flying gravel?

The X4 features tough Corning Gorilla Glass screen protection and comes with removable lens guards designed specifically to protect against flying debris and gravel when riding off-road.

Can I connect an external helmet microphone?

Yes, the camera supports USB-C audio adapters, allowing you to connect wireless or wired helmet microphones to capture crisp vocal commentary while riding.
