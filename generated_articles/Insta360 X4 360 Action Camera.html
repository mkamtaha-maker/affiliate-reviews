Is the Insta360 X4 the Ultimate Camera for Riders?

Motorcycle vloggers and adventure sports enthusiasts in the UK have long sought a camera that captures every angle without requiring a dedicated film crew. The Insta360 X4 delivers exactly that, stepping up the game with headline-grabbing 8K 360-degree recording. Priced at £499.99, this flagship action camera is built to handle high-speed motorway commutes, rugged off-road trails, and everything in between.

Build Quality and Rugged Design

Out of the box, the Insta360 X4 feels reassuringly solid. Designed to withstand harsh road conditions and unpredictable British weather, the X4 features a tough outer shell and comes waterproof down to 10 metres without needing an additional housing. The newly enlarged touchscreen is coated with scratch-resistant glass, offering fluid touch responsiveness even when wearing thin riding gloves.

Stunning 8K Video and Horizon Lock Performance

The true standout feature of the Insta360 X4 is its 8K 30fps 360-degree capture capability. Previous generations suffered from soft reframed HD video, but 8K capture ensures that once you crop into a standard 16:9 frame, your footage remains crisp and detailed. Paired with proprietary FlowState Stabilization and 360 Horizon Lock, video footage remains rock-steady regardless of lean angle, vibrations, or uneven tarmac.

For motorcyclists, the famous Invisible Selfie Stick effect automagically removes the mounting pole from your footage. Mounted to your handlebars or crash bars, it produces jaw-dropping third-person angles that look remarkably like a personal tracking drone follow shot.

Battery Life and Everyday Usability

Power management gets a massive upgrade thanks to the new 2290mAh battery. The camera delivers up to 135 minutes of continuous runtime in 5.7K mode, giving riders ample battery life to record extended road trips through the Scottish Highlands or Brecon Beacons. Fast charging support ensures you can top up during coffee stops.

How It Compares to Alternatives

While traditional action cameras like the GoPro Hero 12 excel at standard wide-angle point-of-view shots, they require precise aiming before you ride. The Insta360 X4 records everything in 360 degrees simultaneously, allowing you to frame the perfect shot during post-production. You never miss an unexpected moment on the road again.

Final Verdict: Should You Buy It?

If you are serious about motorcycle vlogging or capturing high-octane adventure sports in high definition, the Insta360 X4 is a game-changing investment. Despite the slightly larger body and larger file sizes, the incredible 8K quality, rock-solid stabilization, and intuitive editing suite make it the best 360 action camera on the market today.
