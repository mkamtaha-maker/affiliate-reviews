Whether taking tight turns on track day or handling daily highway commutes, safety is non-negotiable. The Shoei RF-1400 relies on Shoei's proprietary Multi-ply Matrix AIM+ shell, combining high-performance fiberglass layers with organic fibers. This produces a shell that is incredibly rigid yet remarkably lightweight. Backed by dual Snell M2020D and DOT certifications, it provides top-tier impact protection designed for modern sportbike speeds.

Wind-Tunnel Refined Aerodynamics & Quiet Acoustics

Shoei engineered every curve of the RF-1400 in their state-of-the-art wind tunnel facility. Compared to older generations, this helmet boasts reduced aerodynamic drag and lift, translating to significantly less neck fatigue during high-speed rides. Additionally, tight shield seals and refined cheek pads significantly reduce wind noise, making it one of the quietest full-face street helmets available.

Crystal-Clear Vision: CWR-F2 Shield & Pinlock EVO

Riders demand distortion-free vision under all weather conditions. The RF-1400 features the CWR-F2 shield system, crafted with a 2D flat outer surface ideal for clarity and tear-off compatibility. Paired with the included Pinlock EVO fog-resistant insert, fogged-up viewports during cold mornings or rain showers are officially a thing of the past. A centralized locking mechanism keeps the shield securely closed at high speeds.

Plush Comfort: 3D Max-Dry Interior II & EQRS

Long trips require maximum comfort. The 3D Max-Dry Interior System II dissipates sweat twice as fast as conventional nylon liners. All interior pads are fully removable, washable, and customizable for a tailor-made fit. In the event of an emergency, first responders can safely remove the helmet with minimal neck tension thanks to the integrated Emergency Quick Release System (EQRS).
